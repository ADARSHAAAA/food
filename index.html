How It Works

Our simple process connects food donors with those who need it most

1

Donate Food

Restaurants, hotels, and individuals can donate surplus food through our easy-to-use platform.

2

We Collect

Our volunteer network picks up the donated food from the specified location at the scheduled time.

3

We Distribute

Food is distributed to shelters, NGOs, and directly to families in need within the community.

4

Track Impact

Donors can track their contribution's impact and receive transparency reports on their donations.
